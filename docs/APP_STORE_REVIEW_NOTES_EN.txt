SUMSN is a Saudi shipping comparison and label-purchase service.

IMPORTANT PAYMENT CLASSIFICATION
The customer payment is for a real-world shipping label and a physical logistics service consumed outside the app. It is not digital content, a subscription, virtual currency, or an app feature. The app therefore uses TuwaiqPay under App Review Guideline 3.1.3(e), which requires payment methods other than In-App Purchase for physical goods and services consumed outside the app. No digital entitlement is unlocked by this payment.

REVIEW STEPS
1. Launch the app and sign in with the review account.
2. On the home screen, enter two Saudi cities, parcel weight, declared value, and dimensions to retrieve live shipping quotations.
3. Select a quotation. Shipment creation requires valid Saudi short addresses and phone numbers.
4. The TuwaiqPay button opens only an approved live HTTPS TuwaiqPay/HypBill host inside an iOS SFSafariViewController. The reviewer does not need to complete a charge.
5. Open the account dashboard. A pre-existing non-sensitive sample label should be available in the review account. Tap “Share or print label” to test the native iOS share sheet, Save to Files, and Print actions.
6. Account deletion is available directly in the account dashboard under “Delete account and data”. A password and explicit confirmation are required.
7. Password recovery and email verification use the customer email address.

NATIVE FUNCTIONALITY
- Native secure payment browser for the real-world shipping service.
- Native PDF validation, protected temporary file handling, and iOS share/print sheet.
- Native quote sharing and haptic feedback.
- Offline connection screen.
